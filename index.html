Why Albania

An undervalued market with rising momentum.

A young, multilingual, tech-fluent population, competitive engineering costs, EU accession progress and a growing remote-work and digital economy make Albania one of Southeast Europe's most compelling frontier ecosystems.

Talent

Strong engineering and design talent fluent in English, Italian and German, at a fraction of Western European cost.

Valuations

Entry prices well below regional peers, with capital-efficient teams that stretch every euro.

Tailwinds

Digital government, EU accession, a thriving diaspora and a fast-growing tourism and fintech base.

Gap in capital

Few dedicated funds exist at seed stage, leaving high-quality founders under-served.

Events

Where investors, founders and partners meet.

We organize free, in-person events in Albania that bring together business leaders, investors, local government, the diaspora and startups. Each event combines panels, Q&A and networking with B2B meetings.
